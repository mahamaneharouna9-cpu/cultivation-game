# Seven-sect NPC hierarchy concepts

## Scope and acceptance

Architecture concept-reference handoff completed before this phase; see SECT_ARCHITECTURE.md for limits. One multiview sheet per rank for all seven sects (63 rank sheets total), followed by support-role studies. Each sheet: front, side, back, face, clothing and equipment. English-only labels, no script-like ornament, distinct rank silhouettes and sect specialties. These are representative rank designs, not a complete roster of named story NPCs or implemented characters. Gender, ages and faces may be adapted when assigning story roles. Garment and equipment consistency still requires modeling review.
